Refund Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Den sells one thing: credit packs that pay for AI 3D generation. This policy explains when you can get your money back.

The policy

Unused credits are refundable within 14 days of purchase.

Email support@dens.world from the address on your account and we will refund the unused portion to your original payment method.

What counts as unused

  • A credit you have not spent on a generation is unused and is refundable within 14 days.
  • A credit spent on a completed generation is used and is not refundable — the generation cost us real money to run and the model is already in your room.
  • If you bought 5 credits and spent 2, you can be refunded for the remaining 3.

Every new account gets 1 free generation before any purchase, so you can judge the quality before you spend anything.

When we refund in full

  • You were charged twice for the same purchase.
  • Credits were not delivered to your account after a successful payment.
  • A generation failed and a credit was deducted anyway.
  • We permanently discontinue Den while you still hold unused credits.

In these cases the 14-day limit does not apply. Contact us and we will fix it.

When we do not refund

  • Credits already spent on completed generations.
  • Dissatisfaction with how a generated model looks. AI output varies; the free generation exists so you can test this first.
  • Credits on an account suspended for breaking the Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Requests made more than 14 days after purchase, unless one of the cases above applies.

How to request one

Email support@dens.world with your account handle and the order number from your receipt. We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds are issued by Creem.io, our Merchant of Record, to your original payment method and typically appear within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Statutory rights

This policy does not limit any refund or cancellation right you have under the consumer law of your own country. If your local law gives you more than this policy does, your local law wins.
