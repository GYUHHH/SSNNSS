Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

This policy explains what Den at dens.world collects, why, and what you can do about it. Den is operated by GyuHyun Park, South Korea. Contact: support@dens.world.

1. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and the handle you choose. Needed to sign you in and to give your room an address.
  • Your room — the furniture you place, styles you pick, books and guestbook entries, and which rooms you follow.
  • Media you upload — photos, music, and video clips you add to your room.
  • Generation prompts — the text prompts and photos you submit for AI 3D generation, and the models produced from them.
  • Purchase records — your credit balance and a record that a payment was fulfilled. We never see or store your card details.

We do not run advertising trackers, we do not sell your data, and we do not build advertising profiles.

2. Why we use it

To run the service: sign you in, store and display your room, run generations you asked for, add credits you paid for, and prevent abuse. That is the whole list. We rely on performance of our contract with you as the legal basis for most of this, and on our legitimate interest in keeping the service safe for abuse prevention.

4. Prompt screening

Text prompts you submit for generation are sent to Creem's moderation service to check them against our Acceptable Use Policy before anything is generated. This is required to keep the service compliant and cannot be turned off while using the generation feature.

5. What other people can see

Your room is reachable at its own address and is intended to be shared. Anything you place in it — photos, music, generated objects, guestbook entries — can be seen by anyone who visits that address. Do not put anything in your room that you would not want a stranger to see. Your email address is never shown to other users.

6. How long we keep it

Room data and uploads are kept while your account exists. Payment fulfilment records are kept for as long as tax and accounting rules require. When you delete your account we delete your room, your uploads, and your generated models.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, or delete it — including deleting your account entirely. Email support@dens.world and we will respond within 30 days. If you are in the EEA or UK you also have the right to object to processing and to complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

Den is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with our providers under their access controls. No service can promise perfect security, but we limit what we collect to what running Den actually requires.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes and material changes are announced in the app.
