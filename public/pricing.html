What Den is

Den is a web app where you furnish a small 3D isometric room. Most of it is free: you pick furniture, arrange it, style the walls and floor, and share the room at your own address.

The only paid feature is AI 3D generation. You describe an object in a short prompt, or upload a photo of one, and Den turns it into a 3D prop placed directly in your room.

The only thing we sell

$3.99 USD — one-time payment for 5 credits

Not a subscription. No recurring charge, no auto-renewal. Tax is added at checkout where required and is shown before you pay.

What you get, and what you don't

  • Generated models are placed in your own room and are yours to use there.
  • They are not downloadable, not transferable between accounts, and not resellable.
  • Nothing is minted, tokenized, or tradeable. There is no secondary market and no payouts to users.
  • A generation that fails to produce a model does not consume a credit.

How the AI works

Den is an independent product with its own interface, built on top of third-party AI models. Generation runs on fal.ai using the Hunyuan3D model. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a reseller of fal.ai or the model's authors; those names are used only to describe what powers the feature.

Den is not an API reseller. You never receive an API key, cannot choose a model, and cannot pass your own parameters. Prompts are limited to 200 characters and are rewritten server-side into a fixed template before generation.

Every prompt is screened before generation. See the Acceptable Use Policy.
