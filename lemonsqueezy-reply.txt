1. WHAT WE SELL

'Den' is a web app where each user decorates a small 3D isometric
room and shares it with friends. Inside the app, users can generate their own
custom 3D furniture from a text prompt or from a photo.

The only paid item is a credit pack that powers those AI generations:

- Credits are a consumable in-app balance. They are not a subscription and
  not a physical good.
- Spending a credit runs one 3D generation on the user's behalf (we use
  fal.ai's Hunyuan3D model) and delivers the finished model directly into
  that user's own room, within about a minute.
- Generated models are for personal use inside the user's own room only.
  They are not downloadable, not transferable between accounts, and not
  resellable. Nothing is minted, tokenized, or tradeable.
- Every new account gets 1 free generation, so buyers know exactly what they
  are paying for before their first purchase.
- Our cost per generation is the third-party model API; the credit price
  covers that plus hosting.


2. PRICING

  Item                        Price       Type
  --------------------------------------------------------
  Credit pack - 5 credits     $3.99 USD   One-time purchase

How credits are spent inside the app:

  - Standard quality generation ........ 1 credit
  - High quality (PBR / glossy) ........ 2 credits

There are no subscriptions, no recurring charges, and no auto-renewal.
Buyers can purchase multiple packs by increasing the quantity at checkout.
Credits are added to the account balance through your order_created webhook.


3. WHO IT IS FOR

Consumers - mainly younger users who enjoy customizing and sharing a
personal space online. It is a hobby and social product, not a B2B tool.
